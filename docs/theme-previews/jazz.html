About

A quintet drawn together by a shared love of the standards and a restless need to push them somewhere new. Velvet Hour has been a fixture of Tokyo's jazz scene since 2021 — from intimate club dates at Cotton Club to late-night sessions at JBS Shimokitazawa, we play for the room and the moment.
