About

Founded in 2019, Sinfonia is a chamber ensemble of eight musicians dedicated to the art of small-scale performance. From Bach to Barber, we seek the beauty in restraint — every note deliberate, every phrase shaped with care. Based in Tokyo, performing across Japan and internationally.
